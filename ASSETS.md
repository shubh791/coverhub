# Ayurvedic Era — Photographic Art Direction & Asset Prompts Guide

This document outlines the visual identity guidelines, asset specifications, and exact Google Flow / Midjourney image generation prompts for all photographic sections across the **Ayurvedic Era** digital experience.

---

## 1. Visual Direction & Aesthetic Pillars

- **Atmosphere**: Serene luxury, grounded botanical wisdom, tactile warmth, reminiscent of luxury retreats like *White Feather*, *Aman*, and the refined editorial calm of *Kinfolk*.
- **Color Palette**: Warm porcelain & ivory (`#FAF7F2`), deep botanical forest green (`#13261D`), terracotta clay (`#B9673C`), and sun-cured brass accents.
- **Lighting**: Soft diffused morning light (golden hour / 8:00 AM sun), delicate architectural shadows cast through natural textures and cane/stone screens.
- **Materials**: Travertine, dark violet biophotonic Miron glass, raw linen, hammered brass, seasoned teakwood, rough terracotta, whole dried botanicals.
- **Strict Avoidance**: No neon or synthetic saturated colors, no cheap plastic packaging, no cartoonish floating leaves, no clinical white pharmaceutical motifs.

## 3. Implementation Workflow

1. Generate images using the prompts above in Google Flow or Midjourney v6.
2. Optimize output into WebP / AVIF at $2 \times$ display density with maximum quality-to-weight balance.
3. Save generated files under `/public/images/{category}/{filename}.webp`.
4. Update `imageSrc` references in `data/products.js` and `data/content.js`.
